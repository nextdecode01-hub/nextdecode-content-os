# Nextdecode: 30-day content and monetization sprint

This is a production and learning sprint for an English-language, global audience.
The target is five original short videos per week, 20 finished packages in 30 days,
and measurable progress toward YouTube Partner Program eligibility. It does not
promise views, approval, or revenue.

## Weekly rhythm

- Monday: research, source checking, topic selection, and benchmark review.
- Tuesday-Friday: produce one video per day using the quality gate below.
- Saturday: finish the fifth weekly video or repair the weakest package.
- Sunday: review the last five videos and choose one experiment for the next week.

## Per-video quality gate

Every package must contain:

1. One specific audience problem or question.
2. At least two source links when the claim is factual, including a primary source where practical.
3. One real screen recording, reproducible test, original diagram, or clearly labelled demonstration.
4. One limitation, uncertainty, or trade-off.
5. A truthful first sentence, one idea, a useful payoff, readable captions, clean audio, and a 9:16 master.
6. Separate YouTube, Instagram, and Facebook copy; no copied scripts, clips, or watermarks.
7. A review entry with the 1-5 rubric, platform metrics at 24 hours and 7 days, and the next hypothesis.

## Monetization track

Track the expanded YPP milestone separately from full ads eligibility. The current
official thresholds are documented in `MONETIZATION_PLAN.md`. Check YouTube Studio's
Earn page for country/account availability, two-step verification, advanced features,
policy status, and AdSense readiness. Do not use paid promotion to inflate metrics.
