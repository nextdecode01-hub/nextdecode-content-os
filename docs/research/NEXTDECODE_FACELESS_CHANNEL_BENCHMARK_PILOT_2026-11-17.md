## Scope and definitions

This is a seed roster, not a claim that 24 channels have been fully audited. The 24 YouTube channel pages were fetched and their page titles matched the listed names. This verifies channel-page identity only; it does **not** independently prove that every recent upload is faceless.

- `verified_title_only`: canonical YouTube channel page returned the expected channel title.
- `provisional_voiceover_led` / `provisional_animated`: format hypothesis supported by secondary research; recent uploads still need direct review.
- `reported_faceless` / `reported_voice_only`: a secondary creator profile explicitly describes a no-camera/voice-only workflow.
- `needs_manual_format_review`: no adequate format evidence captured yet.
- Public metrics are blank. No stale subscriber/view counts or private analytics are inferred.

## Candidate coverage

Business/economics, geography/logistics, engineering, science/space, history, mystery/investigation and natural history are represented. Nature/wildlife is an editorial category only; it is not being used as a proxy for faceless creator format.

## Benchmark protocol — next pass

For each channel, inspect the latest 10 public uploads and capture:
1. Video ID, title, URL, publish date, duration and public views.
2. Presenter-free status; occasional interview or archival people do not automatically disqualify a channel.
3. Hook type, narrative promise, first visual, story structure, payoff, evidence density and visual mix.
4. Shorts vs long-form classification. Never compare raw views across formats or channel sizes.
5. Missing values as null/blank, never zero. Capture date and source URL per observation.

**Target:** 100–150 qualified channels and 1,000+ video observations.  
**Pilot actual:** 24 candidate channels; **0 video-level observations**. No growth-performance conclusion is justified yet.

## Source quality

Directories and commercial “faceless channel” articles are discovery aids, not primary evidence. They can contain stale counts, unsupported RPM claims, affiliate incentives or broad definitions. Prefer YouTube channel/video pages and creator interviews. Public view counts support descriptive comparisons only; CTR, retention, swipe-away, subscriber conversion and revenue are private unless the creator publishes them.
