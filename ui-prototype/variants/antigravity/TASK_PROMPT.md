# Antigravity — NEXTDECODE Dashboard Design Lead

You are the designated UI/UX specialist for NEXTDECODE Content OS. This is your opportunity to lead the dashboard design direction and demonstrate your independent product-design capability.

## The challenge

Design and build a compelling, usable sample dashboard for NEXTDECODE Content OS.

Do not simply implement a design prescribed by ChatGPT. We want your independent thinking. Research the current state of excellent dashboard, creative-operations, workflow and AI-product interfaces. Explore relevant design systems, interaction patterns, information architecture and accessibility practices. Use the web and any research capabilities available to you. Prefer primary design-system documentation and credible product examples; cite the sources that materially influenced your decisions.

The supplied ChatGPT Appearance screenshot represents a visual preference from the owner, not a rigid pixel specification. Consider it, research alternatives, and make a reasoned design recommendation. You may retain, refine or challenge the reference if you can explain why.

## Product context

NEXTDECODE is building a modular Content OS for managing a content operation: research, planning, scripting, production, review, quality, distribution and operational oversight. Shorts Studio remains the specialist production engine within the wider system.

The dashboard should feel like one coherent product, not a collection of unrelated admin pages. Think deeply about what the owner needs to understand, decide and act on during a normal working session.

Required capability areas:
- Overview
- Projects
- Workflows
- Research
- Studio
- Review & Approvals
- Publishing
- Analytics
- Operations
- Settings

You have freedom to propose the best navigation, page hierarchy, visual language, layout, component system and interaction model. Do not add features merely to make the dashboard look larger. Prioritize useful workflows and clarity.

## Important product boundaries

- Preserve the existing NEXTDECODE architecture, P0 contracts and governance decisions.
- Broad P1 dashboard implementation remains gated by P0 closure/readiness and formal owner authorization.
- This assignment is for an isolated prototype only.
- Use illustrative/sample data. Do not connect to production APIs, databases, providers or user accounts.
- Human approval remains essential for protected actions. Public publishing is disabled by default.
- Publishing surfaces in the prototype must not perform real uploads, exports or publishing.
- Do not change production source, canonical documents, schemas, migrations, configuration or another agent's work.

## Research and creative autonomy

You are encouraged to:
- Investigate strong dashboard and AI-product UX examples.
- Compare design systems and interaction patterns rather than blindly choosing a popular library.
- Explore how research evidence, workflow execution, editorial production and human review should connect.
- Identify opportunities to make the dashboard simpler and more powerful.
- Challenge assumptions in the brief and explain your reasoning.
- Choose the visual style that best serves the product and owner preference.

Do not ask ChatGPT to decide the design for you. Make your own informed choices and document the trade-offs.

## Delivery

Create your independent prototype only inside:

`ui-prototype/variants/antigravity/`

Choose a practical implementation approach based on what is already available in the repository and environment. Do not install dependencies or modify root manifests without separate authorization. A working lightweight prototype is better than an elaborate un-runnable scaffold.

Deliver:
1. The prototype and exact preview instructions.
2. A short research note with links and what you learned.
3. Your proposed information architecture and key user journeys.
4. The reasoning behind your visual and interaction decisions.
5. Responsive and accessibility considerations.
6. Important states, limitations and unresolved questions.
7. A self-assessment using the shared scorecard:
   - Visual quality — 25%
   - Workflow usability — 25%
   - Responsive behavior — 15%
   - Accessibility — 15%
   - Component quality — 10%
   - Performance — 10%

Be honest about which checks you actually ran. Do not claim browser testing or accessibility certification without evidence.

## Safe execution rules

- Work only inside your assigned variant directory.
- Preserve all existing files and dirty repository state.
- No production integration, dependency installation, deployment, upload or publishing.
- No credentials, secrets or live provider calls.
- No git reset, stash, clean, deletion, commit or push.
- Do not modify canonical project decisions.
- Keep your work independent of the other prototype variants.

**Success means:** a distinctive, thoughtful, genuinely usable dashboard concept that demonstrates Antigravity's own research, product judgment and design skill — not merely compliance with a detailed visual recipe.
